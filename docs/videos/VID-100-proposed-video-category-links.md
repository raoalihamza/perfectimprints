## How a root was chosen

Each video was given to FIX-911's category resolver (`lib/ai/category-resolver.ts`, slugs and the product names each root actually shows, Patrick's curation included) three ways: the whole title, each colon or dash segment of the title, and each AI topic keyword. The description was NOT fed to it: the resolver takes a product phrase, not prose, and a 500-word description has no single tail to resolve. The second signal is the video's own product strip: each Geiger SKU was looked up in every root's effective product set (baked list, overrides, placements, hides), and each of Patrick's own product pages in the strip was read for its "Add to categories" and "Related category" links.

- **Proposed**: the title (or a keyword) names a root AND the strip's products sit in that root (or the video has no strip and the title rule is one of the strong ones: exact, tail-slug, named).
- **Possible, Ali decides**: one signal only, or the two disagree. Nothing is written for these unless Ali ticks them.
- **None**: the the title resolved to no root and the strip is spread across roots (strategy videos). A video with no row is better than a video on the wrong page.
